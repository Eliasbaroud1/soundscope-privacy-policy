Delete Your SoundScope Account

App: SoundScope · Developer: Olive Works LLC

This page explains how to delete your SoundScope account and the data associated with it.

SoundScope does not use a sign-up form. The first time you open the app, an anonymous account is created automatically so we can provide SoundScope Pro and support. We never collect your name, email address, or a password.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open SoundScope on your device.
  2. Open Settings, then Data & storage.
  3. Tap Delete my account data.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted. This action is irreversible.

Because the account is anonymous and tied to your installation of the app, deleting it from inside the app is the most reliable method.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email us at info@barood.cc. If you can, include your support ID (shown in Settings in the app). Since accounts are anonymous, they are not linked to an email address, so the support ID is how we find yours. We will answer within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Anonymous account record: the anonymous account and authentication tokens stored by our backend provider (Supabase) are permanently deleted.
  • Support ID and app information: your support ID, platform, app version, and dates of use.
  • Subscription record: the record of your SoundScope Pro status, plan, and period end, and any free Pro or support note.

What is not affected

  • Your measurements are stored only on your device and are not affected. Delete them in History, or all of them in Settings → Data & storage → Delete all measurements. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device.
  • Your store subscription keeps running until you cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account. If you keep it, Restore purchases in the app links it to a new anonymous account.
  • Device backups (iCloud, a computer backup, or your Google account backup) are managed by Apple or Google under your account.
Note: request metadata (IP address, timestamps) may be retained by our backend provider for up to 90 days for security and abuse-prevention purposes, after which it is permanently deleted. Crash reports are not linked to your account and are deleted automatically after at most 90 days.

Questions?

Email info@barood.cc with any questions about account or data deletion.
