Getting started

  • Open SoundScope and allow microphone access. There is no sign-up, no email and no password.
  • Tap Start to measure, and Finish to save the measurement to History.
  • In History you can open a measurement to see its chart and statistics, add a title, notes, a place and tags, or export it as a CSV file.

Readings and calibration

  • Phone microphones differ, so out of the box SoundScope shows a loudness score from 0 to 100, not decibels. Louder sounds score higher, and 10 points more means 10 decibels louder on the same phone.
  • To see real sound levels in dB(A), calibrate the app against a reference sound level meter: Settings → Input & calibration. A calibration applies to that phone and microphone.
  • SoundScope is a measuring aid, not a certified sound level meter, and it does not give health advice.

SoundScope Pro

  • Pro removes ads and adds measuring for longer than 1 hour, sleep mode, measuring in the background, a stop timer, dB(C) and dB(Z), microphone choice and sound type recognition.
  • Pro is a subscription, monthly or yearly. New subscribers can start with a 7-day free trial on the Yearly plan; the app reminds you the day before the trial ends (if you allow notifications).
  • Prices are shown in your local currency by the App Store or Google Play before you confirm.

Frequently asked

  • Do I need an account? No. The app creates an anonymous account by itself, so we can recognise your subscription. It has no name, email or password.
  • How do I manage or cancel my subscription? On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, and choose SoundScope. On Android, open the Google Play app, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, and choose SoundScope. Cancel at least 24 hours before your period ends to avoid the next charge. In the app, the Pro screen has a Manage subscription button that takes you there.
  • I subscribed on another device, or reinstalled the app. How do I get Pro back? Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. Your subscription belongs to your Apple Account or Google account, not to one device.
  • How do I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. On iPhone, request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, follow Google's steps at support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637.
  • Does SoundScope record me? No. Sound is turned into level numbers on your device as you measure. Audio is never recorded, saved or uploaded.
  • Where are my measurements? Only on your device, and in your device's own backups. To keep a copy elsewhere, export a measurement as a CSV file.
  • How do I delete my data? Delete measurements in History, or all of them in Settings → Data & storage. To delete your anonymous account and subscription record from our server, use Settings → Data & storage → Delete my account data, or see our Delete account page.
