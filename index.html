Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 28, 2026

1. Introduction

SoundScope ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Olive Works LLC, is a sound level meter for iPhone and Android. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information SoundScope collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information. Olive Works LLC is responsible for the information described here.

By using SoundScope you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

2. Information We Collect

Sound from your microphone. SoundScope uses the microphone only while you are measuring. The sound is turned into level numbers (for example, a level for each second) on your device as you measure. Audio is never recorded, saved, or uploaded. With SoundScope Pro, the app can also show what kind of sound it hears (for example voices or traffic); this recognition runs entirely on your device, and its result is only shown while you measure. If you turn on measuring in the background (Pro), the microphone stays on until you finish the measurement, and your device shows that it is in use.

Your measurements: stored only on your device, never uploaded to our servers:

  • Measurement results: levels, times, duration, and quality notes.
  • What you add yourself: titles, notes, place names, and tags.
  • Calibrations and app settings, including a random installation code that links calibrations to this installation.

Your measurements leave your device only when you choose to export one (you pick where the file goes through your device's share menu), and in your device's own backups (iCloud or a computer backup on iPhone, your Google account backup on Android), which Apple or Google manage under your account.

Anonymous account information: created automatically so we can provide SoundScope Pro and support, stored by our backend provider Supabase Inc. in Frankfurt, Germany:

  • Anonymous account identifier: the first time you open SoundScope, the app creates an anonymous account for you. There is no sign-up: we do not ask for, or collect, your name, email address, or a password. We do not use the device's advertising identifier for this account.
  • Support ID: a short code (for example K7QM-2XDP) shown in Settings and included when you use Contact us, so we can find your subscription when you ask for help.
  • Basic app information: whether the app runs on iPhone or Android, the app version, and when the account was created and last used.
  • Authentication tokens: short-lived tokens that keep your anonymous session active.
  • Subscription status: if you subscribe to SoundScope Pro, the app sends the store's proof of purchase to our server, which checks it with Apple or Google. Apple and Google also notify our server when a subscription renews, is cancelled, has a payment problem, ends, or is refunded. We record the store, the plan, its status, when the current period ends, and the identifier the store assigns to the purchase, all linked to your anonymous account identifier. Apple or Google process the payment; we never see your card or payment details.
  • Free Pro and support notes: if we give you SoundScope Pro for free, we record that and its end date, and we may write a short note while helping you.

For security and abuse prevention, request metadata (IP address, device and app type, timestamp) may be retained by our backend provider for up to 90 days, then permanently deleted. This also applies after you delete your account data: these logs expire on their own.

Advertising (free version only). The free version of SoundScope shows ads from Google AdMob. Before any ad loads, the app shows Google's consent form where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland), and on iPhone, Apple's prompt asking whether the app may track you. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone only if you allow tracking; on Android the advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, information about your device and the app, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this to show, limit, and measure ads, and to personalize them only where you have agreed or the law allows it. SoundScope Pro has no ads: the ad software is not started, and no data is sent to Google for ads.

Crash reports. If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to our error-monitoring provider, Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), stored in the European Union. A report contains technical information only: the app version, your device model and operating system version, the error and where in the app's code it happened, the app screens and steps leading up to it, and a random code for this installation of the app, so we can tell how many installations a crash affects. Crash reports never contain audio, your measurements, or anything you typed, and they are not linked to your anonymous account or support ID. The app also sends anonymous counts of app sessions, so we can see how often the app crashes. Your IP address is not stored with crash reports.

When you contact us. Settings → Contact us opens an email app of your choice, addressed to us. The email is pre-filled with your support ID, the app version, your device model, and its operating system version, so we can help you; you can read or delete these lines before sending. We receive only what you send, and we use it only to answer you.

Notifications and ratings. Notifications from SoundScope (for example while measuring in the background, or a reminder that your free trial is about to end) are created on your device; we do not use a push notification service. If the app asks you to rate it, the rating window belongs to Apple or Google, and we do not learn whether or how you rated.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described in section 2 only to:

  • Measure sound and show, save, and export your measurements on your device.
  • Provide SoundScope Pro: unlock the features you paid for (or that we gave you), and keep them in line with renewals, cancellations, and refunds.
  • Answer your support requests.
  • Show ads in the free version, as described in section 2.
  • Find and fix crashes and errors.
  • Keep our services secure and prevent abuse.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, our legal bases are: performing our contract with you (SoundScope Pro and support); our legitimate interests in keeping the app working and secure (crash reports, request metadata) and in funding the free version with ads; and your consent where it is required (personalized ads and tracking). You can withdraw consent at any time, as described in section 7.

We never use your measurements, notes, or audio for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell or rent your data.

4. Data Storage & Security

SoundScope uses a hybrid storage model:

  • Measurements, calibrations, and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Anonymous account information (section 2) is stored by Supabase Inc. in Frankfurt, Germany, encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Your session is kept in your device's secure storage.
  • Crash reports are stored by Sentry in the European Union, encrypted in transit and at rest.

Google, Apple, Sentry, and Supabase may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where data leaves the European Economic Area or the UK, these providers rely on safeguards recognised by law, such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

5. Third-Party Services

SoundScope integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Supabase Inc. (anonymous accounts and subscription status): supabase.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store (subscription payments on iPhone and iPad; Apple notifies our server about your subscription): apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Play Billing (subscription payments on Android; Google notifies our server about your subscription): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob and Google's consent form (ads in the free version): How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Sentry, Functional Software, Inc. (crash reports): sentry.io/privacy

We integrate no analytics providers or other advertising networks. Your measurements, notes, and audio are never shared with any third party.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your measurements remain on your device for as long as you keep them. You can delete a measurement in History, delete all of them in Settings → Data & storage, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored on your device.

Your anonymous account information is kept while you use SoundScope. You can delete it at any time in Settings → Data & storage → Delete my account data; this permanently deletes the account and everything listed under "Anonymous account information" in section 2. An account with no subscription and no free Pro that has not been used for 180 days is deleted automatically; if you open SoundScope again, it creates a new one. A store subscription keeps running until you cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account. You can also follow the steps on our Delete account page.

Crash reports are deleted automatically after at most 90 days. Data collected by Google for ads is kept under Google's own policies.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable law (including the GDPR, the UK GDPR, and US state laws such as the CCPA) to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy of it.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing of your data.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To change your ad choices, use Settings → Data & storage → Ad privacy choices (shown where Google's consent form applies), your device's tracking settings on iPhone (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking), or your device's ad settings on Android. Because your measurements are stored only on your device, you have full control over them at all times. Because your account is anonymous, please include your support ID (Settings) when you contact us about your account information. To exercise any of the above rights, or for any privacy-related enquiry, contact us at info@barood.cc.

8. Children's Privacy

SoundScope is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us at info@barood.cc and we will take appropriate steps to address the situation.
